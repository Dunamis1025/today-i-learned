### Question 1 — Act, Regulation, Code & Employee Duties

**i) Definitions**

- **Act (Legislation):** A primary law passed by parliament that sets out broad legal principles and mandatory obligations for workplace safety.
- **Regulation:** Subsidiary laws approved under the Act that specify detailed rules, standards, and legal requirements for specific safety hazards.
- **Code of Practice:** Practical guidelines providing actionable advice and industry standards on how to comply with the legal requirements set out in Acts and Regulations.

**ii) In Victoria**

| Category | Legislation / Code Name (Victoria) |
|---|---|
| Act / Legislation | Occupational Health and Safety Act 2004 (Vic) |
| Regulation | Occupational Health and Safety Regulations 2017 (Vic) |
| Code | Compliance Codes (e.g., Workplace amenities and work environment) |

**iii) Employee duties in a virtual environment**

Under the OHS Act, employees have a duty to take reasonable care for their own health and safety and that of others affected by their work, and must cooperate with their employer's safety policies and procedures. When collaborating in a virtual environment, this extends to setting up a safe and ergonomic home workstation, taking regular screen breaks to prevent fatigue, following company remote-work safety policies, and communicating promptly with team members or supervisors about any safety or wellbeing concerns.

### Question 4 — Three Conflict Mediation Methods (15–20 words each)

1. **Active Listening via Video Calls:** Video meetings let team members fully listen to each other, reducing misinterpretation and clearing up misunderstandings quickly.
2. **Focusing on Common Goals:** Remind the team of shared project objectives, encouraging members to compromise and find middle ground on disagreements.
3. **Neutral Mediation by a Team Leader:** When opinions clash strongly, a neutral leader guides fair discussion and makes the final decision.

### Question 5 — Three Constructive Feedback Techniques (15–20 words each)

1. **The Feedback Sandwich Technique:** Start with positive praise, give constructive criticism for improvement, and finish with encouraging words to maintain motivation.
2. **Behavior-Focused Feedback:** Focus strictly on specific work actions or task outcomes rather than criticizing a person's character or personal traits.
3. **Two-Way Discussion:** Encourage an open conversation by listening to the other person's perspective before suggesting practical solutions together.

### Question 16 — SOC/CSOC Definition & Three Components (50–75 words)

A Cyber Security Operations Centre (SOC) is a centralized team that continuously monitors, detects, and responds to cyber security threats across an organization's IT infrastructure. Its three main components are: (1) **People** — skilled security analysts and engineers who monitor systems and respond to incidents; (2) **Processes** — standardized workflows, escalation procedures, and incident response protocols; and (3) **Technology** — security tools such as SIEM platforms, firewalls, and intrusion detection systems.

*(~69 words)*

It provides a structured guideline based on six core functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover. ### Question 20 — Three Basic Firewall Features (20–25 words each)

1. **Packet Filtering:** Packet filtering inspects incoming and outgoing network packets based on predefined security rules to block unauthorized access attempts.
2. **Stateful Inspection:** Stateful inspection tracks active connection states to determine whether network traffic is legitimate, ensuring secure communication sessions.
3. **Access Control Lists / Rules:** Access control lists define specific permissions that allow or deny network traffic based on IP addresses, port numbers, and communication protocols.
