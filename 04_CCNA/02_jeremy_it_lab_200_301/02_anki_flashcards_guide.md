## 1. What Is Anki?

- **Anki** is **SRS (Spaced Repetition Software)**.
- What it lets you do:
  - Create your own flashcards, or **import pre-made decks**.
  - Anki's algorithm **schedules which cards to review each day**, which helps move material into **long-term memory**.
- Is it required for the CCNA? **No**, but it is very useful for memorization, so Jeremy strongly recommends trying it at least once.

## 4. Setting Up Your Decks (Jeremy's Recommended Method)

### Step 1 - Create a parent deck

- After opening Anki, create a **parent deck** (e.g. `CCNA`) to hold everything.

### Step 2 - Import the Day decks

- Click a downloaded Day file (Day 1, Day 2, ...) to import it into Anki.

### Step 3 - Change the daily limits

| Setting | Default | Jeremy's recommendation |
|---------|---------|-------------------------|
| New cards per day | 20 | Raise to something generous (e.g. **999**) |
| Maximum reviews per day | 200 | Raise to something generous (e.g. **999**) |

- Change it via the **gear icon** next to the deck, then **Options**.

### Step 3 - Organize with sub-decks

- **Drag and drop** each imported Day deck onto the parent `CCNA` deck to make it a **sub-deck**.
- To study everything at once, click the parent `CCNA` deck.
- To study one Day only, click that Day's sub-deck.

```
CCNA
├── Day 01 Flashcards - Network Devices
├── Day 02 Flashcards - Interfaces and Cables
└── ...
```

> Renaming a deck to `CCNA::Day 01 ...` (double colon) also nests it under `CCNA`.

## 5. How to Study a Card

### Flow

1. Read the question (usually a **fill-in-the-blank `[...]`** card).
2. **Think of the answer in your head first.**
3. Click **Show Answer**.
4. Choose a rating button based on how well you recalled it.

### Rating buttons

| Button | Meaning | Card returns in |
|--------|---------|-----------------|
| **Again** | You got it wrong / could not recall | Within ~1 minute |
| **Hard** | Correct, but it took a long time | Within ~6 minutes |
| **Good** | Normal correct answer | Within ~10 minutes |
| **Easy** | Very easy | A few days later (shown as ~4-5 days) |

> **Jeremy's advice:** use only **Again** and **Good**. This keeps ratings simple and consistent.
> The exact times shown on the buttons can differ slightly by version and settings.

### How spaced repetition works

- Each time you answer correctly with **Good**, the **interval before the next review grows**.
- Example progression: **1 day -> 3 days -> 5-6 days -> ...**
- Cards you get wrong come back sooner, so your effort goes to the material you find hardest.

## 6. Study Tips and Warnings

- **Do not add too many decks at once.**
  Adding many Days at the same time creates a review pile that is hard to keep up with.
- **Add decks in step with your progress.**
  Import a Day's deck **right after watching that Day's video**.
- **Review every day.**
  Skipping even a day or two lets cards pile up and makes catching up hard.
